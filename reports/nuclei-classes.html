Nucleus classifier classes

The classes each of our models predicts, the per-class scores from its paper, and, for one model per dataset, a confusion matrix we computed ourselves, since no paper reports one: the ten-class PUMA model on the PUMA regions and CellViT-256 on PanNuke. Each runs the released checkpoint on the dataset it was trained on.

F1 score
Harmonic mean of precision and recall, after matching predicted nuclei to annotated ones.
Balanced accuracy
One class against all others: the mean of sensitivity and specificity.
Panoptic quality
F1 score multiplied by how well matched nuclei overlap. It scores finding and outlining together.
All nuclei
Nucleus versus background, ignoring the class.
Matching
Each predicted nucleus is paired with at most one annotated nucleus by optimal assignment of centroids, if the centroids are closer than 3 micrometers. This is the HoVer-NeXt evaluation rule (12 pixels at 0.25 micrometers per pixel).
Share of true class
Each annotated class's row divided by its total, so the diagonal is recall and the missed column is the share never detected.
False positive
A predicted nucleus with no annotated nucleus within 3 micrometers. Its row is shared out over the predicted classes.

Sources: HoVer-NeXt paper (MIDL 2024) · CellViT paper (arXiv 2306.15350) · PUMA dataset paper (GigaScience 2025)
